# Wide screens plan

## Goal
On big screens (above today's frame caps, so ~1600px and up) the app uses the window's width instead of leaving half of it as margin, as picked from the [comparison](https://claude.ai/artifact/6ZehETsEJ5tnhgQA92dwUu). Atlas, the Plan verdict and the Plan chart cards go fluid: the frame follows the window (about 94vw, up to 2400px) and grids fill as many columns as fit, with the ranking table's bars kept near their numbers and the Plan cards going three across once each is still wide enough. The Landing page goes side by side: a wider frame with the story card taking more of the hero, its chart text staying its normal size. The city profile sheet widens too, in three columns under a photo banner that stays pinned and shrinks to a strip once you scroll (option B2 in the [profile comparison](https://claude.ai/artifact/TDpYXu8FXsZtN4hXi6qwT6)). Laptops (≤1440px), tablets and phones look as they do today. Done = checked at 1440, 1920 and 2560px plus a 390px phone, both themes, and it's live.
