# Living on income plan

## Goal
A hidden, experimental mode for retiring the conventional way: living on dividends and bond interest (municipal bonds included), selling shares only to cover the gap. The main site stays pure eVelo. Nothing on it points to the mode. You get in by clicking the landing page's "Find your eVelo" arrow until it gives up and becomes a coupon, or through `#income` once you have it. Inside, it asks one sentence at a time while a chart of income builds layer by layer (dividends, muni coupons, taxable interest, tax, then spending and the gap), and it ends with three measures: the money lasts, principal never sold, income keeps pace. It starts fresh. An opt-in sync copies the shared values you tick from your eVelo plan, once, with a re-sync. After a sync, a side-by-side comparison with eVelo appears: two columns over one chart, with a switch between the measures. Done = all of it works in the built app on desktop and phone, its money logic is proven by tests on the same futures eVelo runs, the main site is unchanged except for the arrow's hidden behaviour, and the mode is live.

- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) VFITX total return uses Yahoo's adjusted close as is, no correction for lump distributions: it matches Vanguard's published calendar-year returns within 0.06 points in all 20 years checked, payout years included; raw distribution events stay unfit as income — from [vfitx-total-return](research/vfitx-total-return.md).
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Income yield = payments per year × median of the trailing 12 months' payments ÷ raw close (12 for the funds, 4 for SPY), so lumps and slipped payments drop out; VWITX reads 5.4% in mid-1993 and 3.1% in mid-2025, not the plan's ≈2.5%, which matches its actual ~$0.035 monthly payout on a ~$13.5 price, so the test bands are 5–7% and 2–3.5%.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) "Reproduces the files" means the same assets, calendar and yields, with returns within one rounding step (1e-5): Yahoo's adjusted closes move in the last digit between requests, so a byte-identical second run isn't possible.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) This stage keeps `fx.json` as committed: a fresh FX refresh moves ALL past the 1% fallback-rate test in `cities.ts`, which is outside the stage; refreshing FX means updating those fallbacks too (open question).
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Where Yahoo's distribution events thin out (VFITX has none from 2025-08, VBMFX lacks 10 of its last 15 months), a month's payout is implied from the adjusted close against the raw close, which the VFITX check proved sound; the refresh logs each such month and fails if the latest 12-month window holds fewer than half the expected payouts — from the stage 1 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) VWITX, VFITX and VBMFX stay out of `market-index.json`, so eVelo's ticker search doesn't show them: the Goal keeps the main site unchanged — from the stage 1 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Stage 3 caps each month's replayed yield change, so a one-off jump in the history can't return as a yield shock — from the stage 1 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) The tax module covers single and married filing jointly; the mode's question picks one in stage 5. The muni sleeve is a national fund, so its income is state-taxed except in no-tax states and DC (and only the in-state share would be exempt, taken as none); AMT and IRMAA are left out, noted in the mode's "How this is worked out" — from [conventional-income-taxes](research/conventional-income-taxes.md): AMT matters little below ~$1M, and a national fund is the honest default.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) State tax is modelled per US home city as the state's 2026 bands and standard deduction/exemptions (single, MFJ) on investment income plus pension, Treasury interest removed and muni interest added where the state taxes a national fund (in-state-exempt and reciprocal states taken as taxing it); a local tax that reaches interest and dividends (NYC, Philadelphia) as a flat rate; Social Security untaxed except where the state taxes it; retirement-income exclusions and credits not modelled. State brackets are treated as indexed (constant in real terms), while federal NIIT and Social Security thresholds stay fixed in nominal terms — keeps the module small for 21 states; contract in `src/engine/incomeTaxTypes.ts`.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) DC is `in-state-exempt`, not all-exempt: D.C. Code § 47-1803.02(a)(1)(B)(ii) taxes other states' muni interest from tax year 2025, reversing the earlier note; the contract gains a per-65+ retirement exclusion (Georgia) and a gains-exclusion share (Missouri's 100%); Washington's gains-only excise, Massachusetts's short-term rate, Hawaii's gains cap and Portland's threshold local taxes stay out, noted per record — from [state-muni-rules](research/state-muni-rules.md).
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) The tax module throws on a `priceLevel` that isn't finite and positive and requires the tax `year` (so the senior deduction's 2029 sunset can't be skipped), treats non-finite or negative incomes as 0, and takes net realised gains (no loss carry-forward); states that exclude pensions (IL, PA, HI) leave them out; Philadelphia's School Income Tax keeps a national muni fund taxed and skips long-term gains, per its regulations §206 — from the stage 2 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) The income engine settles once a year: monthly income by type and price change per sleeve, then at year-end tax, the gap sold pro rata to sleeve values (gains tax solved by iteration, average-cost basis) or the surplus reinvested to the target weights, and no other rebalancing; taxable bonds are half VFITX, half VBMFX; Social Security is the mode's other income; it starts retired, at home (no move, no retire-later) — keeps the questions to one sentence each; [spec](specs/income-engine.md).
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Under eVelo's `fat-tail` method the income engine still uses the bootstrapped months (the t-draw is a portfolio return, not per-sleeve), so the mode is always historical-bootstrap, said in the comparison; yield changes are capped at each asset's 99th-percentile monthly move (SPY 0.27, VWITX 0.13, VFITX 0.25, VBMFX 0.18 points) with a 0.25% floor.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Replayed yield changes are demeaned per asset (history's −1.3 to −2.6 points per 30 years removed), so yields wander around today's level instead of carrying 1993–2026's fall into every future; the 99th-percentile cap applies to the demeaned change — the builder found an all-bond plan's median income falling from $68k to $44k by year 30 from that drift alone; [spec](specs/income-engine.md) §Resolved points.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) Indexed tax amounts lag a year: year k's brackets sit at the price level at the start of the year, not its end, so the engine doesn't hand every path ~3% wider brackets than the law; the `income` worker request also clamps its inputs to eVelo's limits — from the stage 3 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) A double-click on the arrow (two quick clicks, then a pause) goes to the plan like a single click; only three or four quick clicks hold, and the fifth opens the coupon — people who double-click links would otherwise find the main call to action dead; the arrow's link also gets `touch-action: manipulation` so quick taps count instead of zooming — from the stage 4 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) The six questions: (1) saved and yearly spending, with how much of it is what you paid (cost basis); (2) age and years to plan for; (3) the stocks share — draws dividends; (4) the muni share of bonds — draws muni coupons and taxable interest; (5) home city and filing status — draws tax; (6) Social Security amount and start age — draws spending and the gap. The mockups' "federal bracket" question is replaced by home city and filing status, since the engine works tax out from them.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) The chart's "Sold to cover" band is the engine's median `gapSold` (gains tax on the sale included), the same number as the ledger and the summary, so the three never disagree; while a run is working, or after an error, the results from older answers are dimmed and labelled as out of date, and the spending line always comes from the result's own rows — from the stage 5 review.
- 2026-11-18 · (unattended) For the comparison, eVelo's three measures are read from its own result: the money lasts = 1 − brokenRate (solvent at the horizon, the same test the mode uses); principal never sold = successRate (no forced sale and solvent); income keeps pace = 1 − brokenRate, since eVelo funds the full inflation-indexed spending every year until a plan breaks. The shared chart is real net worth. Sync copies spending as the eVelo plan's home spending (today's dollars), since the mode stays home — from `SimulationResult` in `src/engine/types.ts`.
- 2026-11-18 · Income yield starts at each asset's latest real yield and moves by each bootstrapped month's yield change, with a floor; income = yield × value, price change = total return − income. Yield levels are never replayed, so a 1990s block can't pay 6% today — PM default, from [conventional-bond-modelling](research/conventional-bond-modelling.md).
- 2026-11-18 · No separate default or call model for investment-grade munis, and a "ladder" changes only when income arrives, not the returns: fund history already carries both — PM default, from [conventional-bond-modelling](research/conventional-bond-modelling.md).
- 2026-11-18 · The mode holds three sleeves, not tickers: stocks (SPY), munis, taxable bonds, set by a stocks/bonds split and a muni share — PM default: the questions stay one sentence each.
- 2026-11-18 · The mode taxes income by type each year with its own 2026 federal model and per-state muni rules for US home cities; eVelo's flat dividend drag is not used; a non-US home city gets federal tax only, with a note that local tax on interest isn't modelled — PM default, from [conventional-income-taxes](research/conventional-income-taxes.md).
- 2026-11-18 · Three measures: the money lasts (headline, the same test as eVelo's survival), principal never sold, income keeps pace (real income never falls more than 10% below year one) — PM default, from [conventional-income-taxes](research/conventional-income-taxes.md).
- 2026-11-18 · The income engine runs on the same scenarios (seed, path count, blocks) as eVelo, so the comparison is like for like; it's a separate loop, not more branches in `runPaths` — PM default, from the engine survey (the loop is speed-tuned).
- 2026-11-18 · The arrow: a click on the label still goes straight to the plan; a click on the arrow glyph waits 300 ms for a follow-up click before going, and five quick clicks open the coupon instead; keyboard and screen-reader users reach the mode by `#income` only. The coupon stays for this visit (sessionStorage) — PM default: the main call to action keeps working as before.
- 2026-11-18 · The mode keeps its own state under `evelo:v2:income`, parsed like `sanitizeInputs` (typed merge, clamped, own keys); the error boundary's Reset also clears it; no share links for the mode in this plan — PM default: every stored value is a user-input boundary.
- 2026-11-18 · The comparison reads the eVelo plan's own result (the cached run of the plan as it stands); fields that differ from the copy are flagged, never silently re-synced — PM default.
- 2026-11-18 · How the mode works is explained inside the mode ("How this is worked out"), not on the Method page, which would give it away — PM default.
