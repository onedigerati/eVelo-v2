# Stage 1 preview (v30) — checks for the user's logged-in browser

Preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/BQo8DhTKwyWAasaXHLBoth (version 30)

A headless browser can't open the claude.ai page (it needs your login), and Chrome control isn't supported from WSL, so these few clicks on the real page are the last proof that photos load from the artifact's asset store.

1. Open the Atlas. Best odds and Your destination each show **Profile →** beside the city name; click each: the profile opens with five photos.
2. Search **Tokyo**: its row shows a **Profile** chip (not "Top pick"). Click it: the eyebrow says "Profile · Japan" and five photos load. Same for **Austin** ("Profile · United States").
3. Search **Lisbon** and **Volcán** (one from the first upload batches, one from the last): click "Top pick #N", five photos each.
4. Click a city dot on the map: the card has **View profile →** under the choose button.
5. Switch to dark mode and repeat step 1; on a phone, check that Your destination's Profile link sits under the "+N pts" note.

Anything blank or broken: say which city and step.
