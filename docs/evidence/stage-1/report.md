The resting frame matches variation A's Die frame in composition and tokens: the map strip with the arc and the faded home pin and chip; the bars, gains now in the plain stepped-up fill; the loan, ghost and margin lines; the estate column with "At 90 · $19.0M", "Heirs $13.5M" with glyphs, and "Loan repaid $5.5M" lifted off the top; the caption and the note. - St. Louis / Bangkok figures replace Austin / Lisbon.
