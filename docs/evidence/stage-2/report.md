## Against the mockup (by eye)
Each mid-beat frame matches variation A's at the same time:
- Buy: the bars grow with hatched gains, and the destination pin is faint.
- Borrow: the coin drops to the person with "$80K", the loan head sits under the dashed margin line, and the home chip shows.
- Live anywhere: the arc is drawn, a pulse ring appears on Bangkok with its "$47K/yr" chip, and the key's ghost entry fades in.
- Die: the bars fade behind the estate column, "At 90 · $19.0M" sits on top, and the loan slice lifts off with "Loan repaid $5.5M".

The intended differences:
- St. Louis / Bangkok figures replace Austin / Lisbon.
- The key sits below the chart.
- The odds badge switches 63% → 93% at the move's midpoint instead of counting through blended numbers, as check 1 requires.
- There are no step progress bars.

Dark reads well, and the 390 narrow layout keeps every label legible.

A fast Tab→Enter measured the chart while Tab's smooth focus scroll was running and left the card off screen in 2 of 4 presses. Fixed in the review round: the new pure `stepScroll.ts` uses three tiers (chart and step together; else the whole step plus at least half the chart; else the whole chart) in document coordinates, and always issues a scroll that replaces one in flight. That round also moved playback state into a ref, reset the hold when reduced motion changes, gave the IntersectionObserver thresholds 0 and 0.35 (reading the last entry), replaced the StrictMode first-run flag with a prevBeat ref, counted Play as in view when the card is under 35% on screen, and added `LandingStory.tsx`. Final re-verify 2026-11-09: all six checks pass at full depth, there's no bounce at 1280 or 1000 with the mouse or fast keyboard, and StrictMode announces nothing at load ([step-scroll.txt](step-scroll.txt), [live-strictmode.txt](live-strictmode.txt)).
