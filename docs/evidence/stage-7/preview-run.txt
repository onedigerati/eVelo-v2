US
PROFILE · UNITED STATES
A low flat state tax and top-tier hospitals at the foot of the Rockies
Colorado taxes dividends, interest and gains at a flat 4.4% (a graduated tax with up to 8.4% above $500k, Amendment 87, passed on the AP's call in November 2026 but is not yet certified) and has no estate or inheritance tax, so a buy-borrow-die retiree based in Denver keeps most of the portfolio's income and passes it on untouched by the state, with full access to US brokers and securities-based lending. Denver adds several Level 1 trauma centres, an NCI comprehensive cancer centre on the Anschutz campus, the mountains an hour away and one of the world's busiest airports. The costs are housing well above the US average, thin air at 5,280 ft and summer hail and wildfire smoke.

THE ESSENTIALS
TAX ON HOLDINGS
Colorado taxes all income, dividends, interest and realised gains included, at a flat 4.4% (2025 and 2026; TABOR surpluses can trigger a temporary cut, as to 4.25% for 2024); Amendment 87, which passed on the AP's call (about 51.4% yes) in the November 2026 vote but is not yet certified, would from 2027 replace it with six brackets from 3.7% to 4.4% up to $500k of income and 7.4%, 7.9% and 8.4% above $500k, $750k and $1M; Denver levies no income tax on investment income, only an occupational privilege tax of $5.75 a month on employees' wages.
ESTATE & INHERITANCE
No state estate or inheritance tax: Colorado's estate tax was a pick-up of the federal state death tax credit and has been zero for deaths after 2004, so heirs owe only the federal estate tax above the $15M exemption (2026).
LENDING & BROKERS
Full US broker access: every major broker (Schwab, Fidelity, IBKR and others) offers margin and securities-based lines to Colorado residents.
RESIDENCY
No visa needed: a US citizen can live there without any residency process.
HEALTHCARE
UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital on the Anschutz Medical Campus in Aurora (Level 1 trauma, home of the NCI-designated CU Cancer Center) and Denver Health (Level 1 trauma) lead the region, with National Jewish Health for lung disease; Medicare applies from 65.
SAFETY
The US is 134th of 163 in the 2026 Global Peace Index; Denver recorded 37 homicides in 2025, down from 70 in 2024 and 96 in 2021, though the count rose again in 2026 (up about 37% by 1 July), and car theft and property crime are the everyday concerns.
CLIMATE
High, dry and sunny at 5,280 ft (1,609 m): summer highs near 90 °F (32 °C) with afternoon storms and hail, winter snow that usually melts within days; wildfire smoke and ozone alerts recur in summer, and the altitude can tax heart and lungs.
COMMUNITY
A big draw for newcomers from other states; retirees tend to settle in the southern and western suburbs (Highlands Ranch, Littleton, Lakewood) and the foothill towns, with the ski areas and hiking within 1–2 hours.
GETTING THERE
From St. Louis (STL): nonstop to Denver (DEN), about 2–3 h.
Denver International (DEN), a major hub about 40 km north-east of downtown, is linked to Union Station by the A Line commuter train.
Routes as of Nov 2026; flying times are estimates.
WORTH KNOWING
Housing and living costs well above the US average
Altitude, summer hail and wildfire smoke
Amendment 87, if certified, would tax income above $500k at 7.4–8.4% from 2027

US
PROFILE · UNITED STATES
No state income or estate tax, with big retiree suburbs and a world-class airport
Nevada's constitution bars a personal income tax and the state has no estate or inheritance tax, so a buy-borrow-die retiree based in Las Vegas pays nothing to the state on dividends, gains or the estate, and keeps full access to US brokers and securities-based lending. The valley adds large retiree suburbs (Summerlin, Henderson), lower housing costs than California and nonstops worldwide. The costs are thin specialist care, summers that reached 120 °F in 2024 and a water supply that leans on a falling Lake Mead.

THE ESSENTIALS
TAX ON HOLDINGS
No state income tax on dividends, interest or realised gains: the Nevada constitution (Art. 10, §1) bars any income tax on the personal income of natural persons, and there is no city income tax, so only federal tax applies.
ESTATE & INHERITANCE
No state estate or inheritance tax: Nevada's estate tax was a pick-up of the federal state death tax credit, needs no filing for deaths from 2005 and is no longer collected, so heirs owe only the federal estate tax above the $15M exemption (2026).
LENDING & BROKERS
Full US broker access: every major broker (Schwab, Fidelity, IBKR and others) offers margin and securities-based lines to Nevada residents.
RESIDENCY
No visa needed: a US citizen can live there without any residency process.
HEALTHCARE
University Medical Center and Sunrise Hospital (since April 2026) are Nevada's two Level 1 trauma centres, alongside the Valley Health and Dignity Health hospitals and Cleveland Clinic's Lou Ruvo brain-health centre, but Nevada reportedly had the fewest primary-care doctors per capita of any state in 2025 KFF data, and many residents reportedly travel to Los Angeles, Salt Lake City or Phoenix for complex care; Medicare applies from 65.
SAFETY
The US is 134th of 163 in the 2026 Global Peace Index; Las Vegas Metro police recorded 90 homicides in 2025, down about 21% and the first year under 100 since 2019, though the 2026 count was running about 21% ahead of 2025 by late August; the Strip and some central areas see more crime than the suburbs.
CLIMATE
Extreme desert heat: an all-time record 120 °F (49 °C) on 7 July 2024 and a record 36 summer days at 110 °F (43 °C) or more that year, against 9 in 2025 and an average of about 10; winters are mild and sunny, with cold nights.
COMMUNITY
Large retiree communities in Summerlin, Henderson and the Sun City developments, with many newcomers from California; daily life in the suburbs is far from the Strip.
GETTING THERE
From St. Louis (STL): nonstop to Las Vegas (LAS), about 3–4 h.
Harry Reid International (LAS) sits next to the south end of the Strip, a short drive from downtown, and reaches about two dozen international markets besides most of the country.
Routes as of Nov 2026; flying times are estimates.
WORTH KNOWING
Thin specialist care; complex cases often go out of state
Very hot summers, with a record 120 °F in 2024
About 90% of the valley's water comes from Lake Mead, under a Colorado River shortage

US
PROFILE · UNITED STATES
No state income or estate tax, in the US capital of Latin America
Florida has no state income tax and no estate or inheritance tax, so a buy-borrow-die retiree based in Miami pays nothing to the state on dividends, gains or the estate, and keeps full access to US brokers and securities-based lending. Miami adds big academic hospitals, a subtropical winter, beaches and the main US airport for Latin America and the Caribbean. It is also the dearest city in Florida: housing, home insurance and flood risk cost far more than elsewhere in the state, and hurricanes are a real threat.

THE ESSENTIALS
TAX ON HOLDINGS
No state income tax on dividends, interest or realised gains: Florida's constitution (Art. VII, s. 5) bars a state income tax on natural persons, and the Department of Revenue administers no personal income tax; there is no city income tax either, so only federal tax applies.
ESTATE & INHERITANCE
No state estate or inheritance tax: Florida's estate tax ended for deaths after 31 December 2004, when the federal credit it was tied to was phased out, and the constitution bars any inheritance tax beyond that credit, so heirs owe only the federal estate tax above the $15M exemption (2026).
LENDING & BROKERS
Full US broker access: every major broker (Schwab, Fidelity, IBKR and others) offers margin and securities-based lines to Florida residents.
RESIDENCY
No visa needed: a US citizen can live there without any residency process.
HEALTHCARE
Jackson Memorial (with the Ryder Trauma Center, a Level 1 centre), University of Miami Health and its Sylvester cancer centre, Baptist Health South Florida and Mount Sinai are all in the metro, with Cleveland Clinic Florida in nearby Weston; Medicare applies from 65.
SAFETY
The US is 134th of 163 in the 2026 Global Peace Index; the City of Miami recorded 27 homicides in both 2024 and 2025, close to its record low of 24, and 8 in the first half of 2026 against 16 a year earlier, though property crime and some neighbourhoods remain a concern.
CLIMATE
Warm and dry from November to April, then hot, humid and rainy from May to October; hurricane season runs June to November, and low-lying areas already see tidal 'sunny-day' flooding as sea levels rise.
COMMUNITY
A large, international metro where about 54% of the county's residents were born abroad (2024) and Spanish is as common as English; retirees and newcomers from the Northeast cluster in Coral Gables, Brickell, the beaches and Broward and Palm Beach to the north.
GETTING THERE
From St. Louis (STL): nonstop to Miami (MIA), about 2–4 h.
Miami International (MIA), the main gateway to Latin America and the Caribbean, is about 13 km west of downtown, linked by Metrorail; Fort Lauderdale (FLL), about 45 km north, adds low-cost routes.
Routes as of Nov 2026; flying times are estimates.
WORTH KNOWING
Home insurance among the dearest in the US (roughly $6,000 a year for a typical Miami-Dade house by private estimates, 2026), plus flood cover; Citizens cut Miami-Dade rates about 14% from June 2026
Property tax of about 2% of assessed value inside the city (20.0 mills, 2025); homesteads get about $50k off and a 3% yearly cap on assessed value, and voters approved Amendment 3 on 3 November 2026 (about 62%, unofficial results; the state certifies on 17 November) to raise the exemption from non-school tax to $150k in 2027 and $250k in 2028, though owners who were not Florida residents on 31 December 2026 get the larger exemption only from their fifth year of eligibility
Hurricanes, sea-level rise and the highest housing costs in Florida
