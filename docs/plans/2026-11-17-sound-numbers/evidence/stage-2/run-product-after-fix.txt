== [desktop] Checks 4/5: Method page ==
currency paragraph: Then the currency. Prices abroad are set in local money, so destination spending is multiplied by a real exchange-rate random walk: each month it takes a small random step, sized so that over a year it swings by the city’s volatility (THB 7% a year for Bangkok; zero for dollar economies), and centered so the typical future keeps today’s rate. Some futures get a strong dollar and a cheap life; others the opposite, for decades. The swings don’t even out: a bad run compounds into decades of dear living, so a volatile currency costs odds. The move can happen today or years from now, but never before you retire.
