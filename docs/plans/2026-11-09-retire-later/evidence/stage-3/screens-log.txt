method paragraph: Retiring later. You can retire today (the default) or in up to 15 whole years. Until then each month is a working month: nothing is drawn for spending and other income isn’t counted. Instead your savings while working ($0 a year by default, kept level with inflation) go in. They pay down any loan first and the rest is invested, raising the cost basis. The loan still accrues interest and the lender still checks every month. An opening forced sale still happens today, before any savings arrive. From retirement on, the months run as above, and other income starts then, or at its own start age if that is later. The plan still ends at the same age, so retiring later leaves fewer years of drawing. The safe-spending search scales retirement spending only, never the savings.
