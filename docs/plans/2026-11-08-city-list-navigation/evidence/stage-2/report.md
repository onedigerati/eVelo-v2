## Notes (not failures)
- **Blur fix (found here, pre-existing, app-wide).** Production CSS kept only `-webkit-backdrop-filter`, which Chromium 143 doesn't support, so no glass surface blurred in Chrome/Edge. Cause, reproduced with lightningcss directly: with the unprefixed declaration *first*, the minifier collapses the pair into the prefixed form; with the prefixed one first it keeps both. Fixed by putting `-webkit-backdrop-filter` first in all 12 pairs; the build now emits 12 of each, and in Chromium on `#atlas` 5 elements compute a non-`none` `backdrop-filter` (was 0). Tests 387/387 after the change. This also removes the faint ghosting of map labels under the hover card noted by the verifier.
- Playwright's `page.fill` makes headless Chromium drop the emulated coarse pointer, so [browser-checks.txt](browser-checks.txt) measures phone chips at 34px after its search steps; 44px is judged at load and in the fresh touch page ([phone-chips.txt](phone-chips.txt)).
- On phones, when the docked card opens near the bottom of the screen the tab bar covers its actions until the page is scrolled. Unchanged by this stage.
- Map chips show fixed totals (they don't follow the list search); the plan only asks the list chips to.
