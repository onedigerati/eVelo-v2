TOP PICK 16 OF 75 · PORTUGAL
Portugal's BBD tax profile, spring-like all year
Madeira has Portugal's buy-borrow-die strengths (no wealth tax, children inherit free, a D7 visa that accepts dividends, 7th in the Global Peace Index) and one of Europe's mildest climates, warm and even all year. It is an island with one main hospital and limited flights. The weak point is the borrow step: Schwab and IBKR both reportedly bar EU residents from drawing margin as cash.

THE ESSENTIALS
TAX ON HOLDINGS
No wealth tax on financial assets; dividends and interest pay Portugal's 28% flat rate, with US tax on them creditable up to the treaty caps (15% on dividends, 10% on interest); Madeira's regional cuts (30% below mainland rates in 2026) apply to the progressive scale and to the flat rate withheld on Portuguese-paid interest and dividends, but whether they reach the 28% on dividends from a US broker is unverified; NHR is closed; check current rules.
ESTATE & INHERITANCE
No inheritance tax as such: a 10% stamp duty reaches only assets located in Portugal, and spouses, children and grandchildren are exempt, so US brokerage assets left to US-resident children appear untaxed locally; there is no US–Portugal estate treaty, and the $15M US exemption (2026) applies as anywhere.
LENDING & BROKERS
IBKR reportedly accepts Portugal residents (likely through its EU entity), and Schwab International reportedly accepts them too, but Schwab reportedly bars Europe residents from taking cash out through margin, and IBKR's EU entity reportedly lends on margin only to buy securities, not to withdraw as cash, so confirm how you would draw cash before relying on either; EU PRIIPs rules reportedly block new buys of US-listed ETFs, not holding them.
RESIDENCY
D7 passive-income visa: at least €920/month (the 2026 minimum wage; +50% for a second adult) from dividends, interest, rent or pensions, plus about €11,040 of savings; loan draws are not on the consulates' list of income and are believed not to count, and AIMA processing backlogs are common.
HEALTHCARE
Legal residents can register with SESARAM, the regional public service (main hospital: Dr. Nélio Mendonça in Funchal); the private Hospital CUF Madeira (the former Hospital Particular da Madeira) and Hospital da Luz Funchal have English-speaking staff, and highly specialised care generally means a transfer to Lisbon or Porto.
SAFETY
Portugal is 7th in the 2026 Global Peace Index; Funchal has very low crime, and the main risks are steep roads, wildfires and landslides after heavy rain.
CLIMATE
Subtropical and very even: days roughly 19–20°C in winter and 25–26°C in summer, drier and sunnier on the south coast around Funchal.
COMMUNITY
A smaller English-speaking scene than the Algarve, mostly British and German retirees plus a growing remote-worker crowd.
GETTING THERE
From St. Louis (STL): no nonstop; one stop via Frankfurt, about 15–17 h.
Madeira airport is on the east coast, about 20 minutes' drive from Funchal; Lisbon is under 2 hours by air, and a ferry runs to Porto Santo.
Routes as of Nov 2026; flying times are estimates.
WORTH KNOWING
One main public hospital, and complex care can mean a flight to Lisbon
Year-round US trips mean a connection; the Newark nonstop is summer-only
28% on dividends and interest, and Schwab and IBKR both reportedly bar drawing margin as cash

Faro airport, just west of Faro, serves the whole Algarve coast; Lisbon, with a far wider choice of flights, is about 2.5–3 hours by motorway or train.
